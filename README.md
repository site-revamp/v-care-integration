# V Care Integration Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

V Care Integration Pte. Ltd. provides building services, technical system integration, installation, and maintenance support in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps residential and commercial property owners, businesses, and property managers find the relevant service and request assistance.

## Main Improvements

1. **Organise the broad service offering.** The proposed homepage would group technical systems and ongoing maintenance into clear starting points. Visitors could quickly find electrical work, security and access systems, communications and networking, solar and EV charging, or general property maintenance.
2. **Rewrite service descriptions for clarity.** Replace awkward wording and generic explanations with concise descriptions of the work VCI provides. Consistent terminology and corrected grammar would make its technical capabilities easier to understand.
3. **Give company information and enquiries greater visibility.** Consolidate repeated service and pricing content to make room for company details, supported team capabilities, and a clear consultation-to-maintenance process. Prominent phone, WhatsApp, and email actions would connect visitors to the team from relevant service sections.

## Design Improvements

Clear headings, consistently structured service summaries, and concise process steps would make the homepage easier to scan. Enquiry actions placed beside relevant services would give visitors a direct next step.
