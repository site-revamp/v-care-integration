About VCI

Bringing essential building services together.

V Care Integration Pte Ltd supports residential and commercial customers with a broad range of technical systems and property services.

Its service offering spans electrical works, CCTV and card access, audio-visual and public address systems, networking, intercom, IP clock systems, solar solutions, EV charging and maintenance support.

By bringing these capabilities under one service partner, VCI aims to make it easier for customers to discuss their requirements, plan work and arrange the support their properties need.
